EMERGENCY MODE

Need immediate help?

Use the button only if you need to record an emergency event. Confirm before activation. The app cannot contact emergency services.

WHAT HAPPENS

Know before you activate

  • Your emergency event and available coordinates are saved to your account.
  • Development/test mode never sends messages. In production, a configured Twilio service attempts SMS to your saved contacts.
  • A provider's acceptance of an SMS does not confirm delivery.
  • This app cannot call emergency services. Contact your local service directly for immediate danger.
